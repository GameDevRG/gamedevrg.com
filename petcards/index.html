Daily Pet Packs

Five real adoptable animals, free every day

Every animal comes from a local shelter, and every one has exactly the same chance of showing up. No animal is rarer than another; only the sparkle is luck of the draw. Open a pack, enjoy the reveal, and add the ones that make you smile to your favorites. Every card links to its shelter so you can go meet them for real.

Your collection

Sample data: these pets and shelters are made up to show the design, and the “Meet” links are placeholders. The live game would use real RescueGroups.org listings (name, photo, description and the shelter’s own adoption page). A hosted page can’t call that API directly because it needs a private key, so live listings need a small backend. Sparkle finishes vary in rarity, but they’re random and independent of the animal: any pet can come with any finish, and every animal is equally likely to appear. Icons: Material Design Icons by Pictogrammers (Apache 2.0).
