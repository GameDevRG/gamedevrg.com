about this webpage

every critter is a real wild animal, spotted and photographed by a nature lover and shared on iNaturalist, and every one has exactly the same chance of showing up. no critter is rarer than another; only the sparkle is luck of the draw, and sparkle is rare: most cards are plain, about 1 in 4 has a tinted card color, about 1 in 3 has a parallax photo, about 1 in 16 is full art (the photo fills the whole card), about 1 in 12 has an image effect (toon, pixel art, noir and more) that changes the photo itself, about 1 in 4 is slightly shiny, about 1 in 10 has a special finish, and roughly 1 in 100 has a super rare animated one. open a pack, enjoy the reveal, and add the ones that make you smile to your favorites. every card links to its original sighting on iNaturalist. trade in cards you don’t need for points, then spend the points on extra packs in the pack shop. the critter always stays in your list of critters you’ve met, so you can still look it up.
