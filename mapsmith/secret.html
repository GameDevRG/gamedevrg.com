mapsmith

shortcuts

click select · shift+click add/remove
shift+drag or select button: box select
drag pins, trees & crosswalks to move them
drag a selected shape to move it
drag a road end onto a freeway or highway to merge it into the traffic (alt = don't attach)
click a rounded junction corner to resize it
click a road point: bezier handles (shift = snap angle, alt = one side only)
←↑↓→ nudge (shift = bigger)
Ctrl+D duplicate · Del delete
Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Shift+Z redo
V isometric 3D view (drag = pan, right-drag or shift+drag = rotate, Q/E = turn 90°)
F fit map · G grid · Space+drag pan
Esc deselect / cancel
