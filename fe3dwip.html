fractal type

what is this?

each shape below is a distance-estimated surface, sphere-traced from the camera every frame. mandelbulb and mandelbox are escape-time formulas generalized to 3d, sierpinski, menger and the folding ifs are exact folded constructions, quaternion julia, burning ship and julia bulb are more escape-time formulas, the apollonian gasket is a sphere-inversion packing, and custom lets you write your own.

custom equation

what is this?

write your own iteration step, applied to an ordinary 3d point z, starting from z = position. it's compiled to a gpu shader and drawn live, the same way the 2d engine compiles complex equations — but here every operator works component by component on x, y and z, and the distance to the surface is estimated numerically rather than analytically, so it's an approximation: some formulas may need the precision or iteration count tuned to look their best.

custom formulas only get an approximate distance estimate, which can occasionally overshoot and tear the surface. lowering step scale takes smaller, safer steps — slower to render, but cleaner. if you see torn or noisy patches, especially when zoomed out, try turning this down.

syntax reference

each step repeats z ← f(z), starting from z = position. everything is an ordinary 3d vector.

names: z current vector, c the fixed position, pi, e, plus any parameters you add below

operators: + − * / ^, all applied component by component, plus implicit multiplication (2z, a(z+c))

functions: sin cos tan sinh cosh tanh exp log sqrt abs (component-wise), sqr (z*z), recip (1/z), mag (length, broadcast to all three axes), boxfold and spherefold (the two mandelbox-style folds), plus floor ceil fract sign

more folds (each takes and returns a vector): tetrafold (sierpinski tetrahedron mirror folds), octafold (abs then sort the axes, the octahedron / menger fold), sortfold (sort the axes largest first), invert (sphere inversion, z / |z|²), kalifold (|z| / |z|²), ballfold (inverts only inside the unit sphere), cubefold (box fold without the sphere part), fractfold (repeat space in a grid, apollonian style), cyclefold (rotate the axes x→y→z), and foldx foldy foldz (mirror one axis)

try: boxfold(z)*a + c, abs(z)^a + c, sin(z)*a + c, spherefold(boxfold(z))*a + c, tetrafold(z)*2 - a, octafold(z)*3 - a, kalifold(z)*a + c

post-processing

what is this?

a second pass over the finished picture, run as its own gpu shader: bloom lets bright highlights glow softly into their surroundings, light rays cast a volumetric shaft of light out from the sun through anything blocking it, depth of field blurs whatever is nearer or farther than a chosen focus distance, and there's a vignette, chromatic aberration and film grain on top of that. filmic tonemapping changes how the raw brightness is rolled off into a displayable image before any of this is applied. turning the whole thing off restores the plain, uncorrected picture.

camera

what is this?

the camera orbits the origin. drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, or turn on auto-rotate to let it spin on its own. switch to free fly to pilot the camera by hand — wasd (or a joystick on touch) to move, drag to look around, and scroll or the speed slider to change how fast you fly. barrel roll is a flight simulator: the camera flies like a plane, steered by where your pointer sits relative to the middle of the view, and it can turn all the way over.

2d slice

what is this?

cuts the fractal with a flat plane and draws the intersection as a flat 2d image, brightened wherever the surface passes close to that plane. the view smoothly splits so the slice appears beside the 3d view, and the cutting plane itself is drawn in the 3d view so you can see exactly where it sits. drag the 2d view to move the plane, shift-drag (or the rotate button on touch) to spin it, scroll or pinch to zoom, and drag the divider between the two views to resize them.
