Pick a material and draw (keys 0-9 pick materials, Space pauses, . steps). Open Code to rewrite how it behaves.

















Drag blocks from the palette into the script, or tap one to add it at the end. Drop round blocks into white slots and pointed ones into pointed slots. Drag a block (and everything under it) onto the palette to delete it. Blocks dropped away from the script stay loose and will not run until attached under the hat.
