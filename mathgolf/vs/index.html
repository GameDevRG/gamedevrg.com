    match
    game mode
    teamspoints and grid modes: red vs blue. a team's score is its players' points (or levels in the grid). you can move players between teams here in the lobby
    roundshow many levels the match lasts (points mode: how many rounds)
    endless modepoints only: no set number of rounds. the match lasts as long as you pick below and the most points wins
    match lengthendless mode only: how long the whole match lasts
    grid widththe grid: how many columns of levels
    grid heightthe grid: how many rows of levels
    grid time limitthe grid only: how long the match lasts. with no limit it goes on until the host ends it
    show everyone's progressrace mode: players see how many levels everyone has finished. turn it off to race blind. (points mode always shows the scores)
    end when someone finishesrace mode: the match ends as soon as the first player finishes every level, and they win. anyone still playing is marked as not finished
    replay solutionspoints only: when someone scores, everyone watches their solution play out before the next level. longer solutions play faster
    levels
    difficultyhow hard the levels are
    random difficultiesevery level gets its own difficulty. in the grid, squares get an outline in that difficulty's colour: green easy, yellow medium, red hard, blue challenge
    favoured difficultyrandom difficulties only: pick one to see it more often, or leave it on truly random
    difficulties in the mixrandom difficulties only: tap a difficulty to leave it out of the random pick (crossed out = disabled). at least one stays on
    numbersuse whole numbers, decimals or fractions
    decimal goalsdecimals only: the goal can be a decimal like 3.5
    fraction goalsfractions only: the goal can be a fraction like 7/4
    roots of non-squaresallow √ on numbers that aren't perfect squares. always on for fractions
    game packsevery level comes from one of the packs that are on. base game: the familiar operations. complex numbers: numbers with an imaginary part, played on a plane. the numbers, decimal goals and roots settings only change base game levels. at least one pack stays on
    sand pitslevels get sand pits: numbers, or a whole stretch of numbers, that you can land on but not move from, so you can only undo. they are placed to block some routes, and every level can still be solved
    scoring
    weighted pointspoints and grid modes: harder levels are worth more points. set the points below
    easy pointsweighted points: what a easy level is worth
    medium pointsweighted points: what a medium level is worth
    hard pointsweighted points: what a hard level is worth
    challenge pointsweighted points: what a challenge level is worth
    rules
    time limithow long each round can last
    moves over parhow many moves over par a player may use on one level. out of moves? undo or restart the level
    room
    max playershow many people can be in the room, including the host
    host playsturn off to only watch: you get a live scoreboard screen to show everyone
    profanity filterhides swear words in chat and blocks them in names
    calculatorsplayers can open a calculator window during the match. turn off for a pure mental-math game
    how to play screenbefore the match starts, everyone sees a short how-to-play screen. only the host can continue
