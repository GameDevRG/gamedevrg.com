every level is freshly generated and gives only the operations you need. par is a number of moves that is guaranteed to work (on challenge there is often a shorter route, so you can beat par), and you can keep going past it, but every extra move is one over par. many levels have more than one route. in hardcore mode (settings) you only get 3 undos per level, can't restart, and can't go over par. by default levels use whole numbers, and division only works when the result is whole, so a greyed-out ÷ button means your number doesn't divide evenly. challenge difficulty adds extra operations like |x| (absolute value), √x, x³, negate, mod and digit reverse, and has lots of different solutions. in settings you can switch to decimals (moves like +0.5, and x⁻¹ turns 4 into 0.25) or fractions (division can leave fractions, and x⁻¹ turns 4 into 1/4). in decimals mode you can also allow decimal goals.
