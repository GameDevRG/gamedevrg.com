every level is freshly generated and gives only the operations you need. par is the fewest moves that can work, and you can keep going past it, but every extra move is one over par. many levels have more than one route. in hardcore mode (settings) you only get 3 undos per level and can't restart. division only works when the result is a whole number, so a greyed-out ÷ button means your current number doesn't divide evenly. if you turn on decimals & reciprocals in settings, levels can also use decimal moves like +0.5, division may give a decimal, and x⁻¹ turns a number into its reciprocal, so 4 becomes 0.25.
